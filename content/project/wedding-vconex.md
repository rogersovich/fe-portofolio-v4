> ### **Project Background**

The "Wedding Vconex" website was designed as a modern, interactive digital wedding invitation and information hub. It solves the challenge of traditionally disseminating wedding details by providing a centralized, elegant, and eco-friendly platform for couples to share their love story, ceremony details, and gather RSVPs. Its primary objective is to enhance the wedding experience for guests by providing all necessary information in one accessible place and adding interactive elements to celebrate the couple's journey.

> ### **Key Features**

The "Wedding Vconex" website is packed with features to make a couple's special day memorable and accessible:

- **Save the Date Section:** Prominently displays key dates and information for guests to mark their calendars.

- **Ceremony Showcase with Live Comment:** Provides details about the wedding ceremony and potentially offers a live comment feature for guests to share well wishes and interact in real-time during the event.

- **Love Story Page:** Dedicated section for the couple to share their journey, how they met, and significant milestones, adding a personal touch for guests.

- **Invite Code Form:** Ensures privacy and manages access, allowing only invited guests to view specific event details by entering a unique code.

- **RSVP Form:** Simplifies the process for guests to confirm their attendance, including meal preferences or plus-one information.

- **Photo Gallery:** A beautiful space to display cherished moments of the couple, pre-wedding photos, or even post-wedding highlights.

> ### **Tech Stack**

This project was built with a modern and efficient frontend stack, focusing on dynamic and interactive user experiences:

- **Frontend:** Vue.js, Tailwind CSS

> ### **My Role & Contribution**

As a **Frontend Developer** on the "Wedding Vconex" project, I was responsible for bringing the visual designs to life and implementing all the interactive features. My work involved developing the various pages, integrating the forms, setting up dynamic content displays, and ensuring a seamless and visually appealing experience for all guests across different devices.

> ### **Lessons Learned & Growth**

Developing the "Wedding Vconex" website was a delightful and instructive experience, allowing me to significantly grow my skills:

- **Mastery of Vue.js for Interactive UIs:** I deepened my expertise in using Vue.js to create dynamic and engaging user interfaces, particularly in handling forms, conditional rendering, and interactive components like the live comment section.

- **Efficient Styling with Tailwind CSS:** I further honed my skills in leveraging Tailwind CSS for rapid and consistent styling, allowing for highly customizable and responsive designs without extensive custom CSS.

- **User Flow and Experience Design:** This project reinforced the importance of understanding user journeys, especially for an event-based website, to ensure intuitive navigation and clear information presentation.

- **Form Handling and Validation:** I gained practical experience in building robust forms and implementing client-side validation to ensure smooth data collection for RSVPs and invite codes.

- **Attention to Detail in Visual Presentation:** Crafting an aesthetically pleasing wedding website demanded meticulous attention to design details, from typography and color palettes to image presentation, to create an elegant and memorable experience.
