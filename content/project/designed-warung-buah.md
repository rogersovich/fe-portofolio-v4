> ### **Project Background**

The "Warung Buah" project was initiated as a self-learning endeavor to explore and master UI/UX design principles. The core problem it addresses is the need for a user-friendly and aesthetically pleasing online platform for buying fruits. The main objective was to design a seamless and enjoyable shopping experience, from Browse products to completing an order, demonstrating best practices in marketplace application design.

---

> ### **Key Features**

"Warung Buah" showcases a comprehensive set of features crucial for an e-commerce platform, all designed with user experience in mind:

- **Home Screen:** Provides an attractive and easy-to-navigate entry point for users to discover various fruits.

- **Product Detail Page:** Offers comprehensive information about individual fruits, including images, descriptions, and pricing.

- **Add to Cart Functionality:** Allows users to easily add desired fruits to their shopping cart from both the home screen and product detail page.

- **Shopping Cart:** A clear and organized view of selected items, with options to adjust quantities or remove items.

- **Order Page:** Facilitates the checkout process, guiding users through payment and delivery options.

- **Category-Based Browse:** Enables users to explore fruits by specific categories, enhancing discoverability.

- **Filter Options:** Provides tools for users to refine their search within categories based on various criteria.

- **Advanced Search Functionality:** Allows users to quickly find specific fruits using keywords.

This project was exclusively a design endeavor, focusing on visual and interactive elements:

- **Tools:** Figma

> ### **My Role & Contribution**

This project was a **personal learning initiative** where I took on the role of a **UI/UX Designer**. I was solely responsible for the entire design process, from ideation and wireframing to creating high-fidelity mockups and interactive prototypes. It was an immersive experience in understanding user needs and translating them into a functional and visually appealing design.

---

> ### **Lessons Learned & Growth**

The "Warung Buah" project proved to be an invaluable journey in my growth as a designer:

- **Foundational UI/UX Design Skills:** I gained a solid understanding of core UI/UX principles, including user flow, information architecture, visual hierarchy, and interaction design.

- **Figma Proficiency:** My skills in Figma significantly deepened, allowing me to efficiently create detailed designs, prototypes, and reusable components.

- **User-Centric Thinking:** I learned to approach design with a strong user-centric mindset, constantly considering how users would interact with the application and how to optimize their experience.

- **Problem-Solving Through Design:** This project challenged me to solve practical design problems, such as simplifying complex checkout flows and ensuring clear navigation.

- **Attention to Detail:** I developed a keen eye for detail in visual design, focusing on typography, color palettes, spacing, and iconography to create a polished and professional interface.
