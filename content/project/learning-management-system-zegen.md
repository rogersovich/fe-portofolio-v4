> ### **Project Background**

This project was initiated to address common inefficiencies in managing academic processes within educational institutions. Our primary goal was to create a centralized, intuitive, and efficient system that empowers administrators to manage student data, academic schedules, assignments, and financial records with ease. The application aims to foster a more organized and transparent learning environment for students and educators alike, moving away from fragmented, manual processes to a unified digital solution.

---

> ### **Key Features**

The **Learning Management System** is equipped with a comprehensive set of functionalities designed to optimize various aspects of school administration and student engagement:

- **Interactive Dashboard:** Provides administrators with a high-level overview of key metrics, including student attendance summaries, school activity schedules, and important dates (holidays, exams).

- **Student Task & Assignment Management:** Facilitates the creation, assignment, and tracking of student tasks and homework. It allows for detailed task descriptions, assignment to specific classes/students, and monitoring of submission status.

- **Dynamic Learning & Exam Schedules:** Enables the creation and display of daily learning schedules and examination timetables (e.g., UAS). These schedules are class-specific and provide clear information on subjects, times, and instructors.

- **User & Role Management:** Securely manages different user types (e.g., Admin, Teacher, Student) with appropriate access levels and permissions.

- **School Information Hub:** Centralized portal for sharing important announcements, school holidays, and event details.

- **Financial Overview (Admin-side):** Presents a comprehensive revenue and expense report, allowing for financial tracking, import/export capabilities, and filtering of financial data.

- **Student Registration & Data Management:** Streamlines the process of adding new students and managing their records, including personal and family information.

---

> ### **Tech Stack**

As a **Frontend Developer** on this project, I primarily focused on crafting a highly responsive and engaging user interface. The robust architecture behind this system relies on a modern tech stack:

- **Frontend:**

    - **React.js:** Utilized for building dynamic and interactive user interfaces, ensuring a component-based and scalable frontend.

    - **React Redux:** Employed for efficient state management across the application, ensuring predictable data flow and easier debugging.

    - **Chakra UI:** Integrated to provide a highly customizable and accessible component library, accelerating UI development while maintaining a clean, consistent, and user-friendly design.

- **Backend:**

    - **Golang:** The powerful and efficient backend language was used to build robust and high-performance APIs, handling business logic and data processing.

- **Database:**

    - **PostgreSQL:** Chosen as the relational database management system for its reliability, data integrity, and strong support for complex queries, efficiently storing and managing all application data.

> ### **Lessons Learned & Growth**

This project was an invaluable opportunity to grow as a Frontend Developer. I gained significant experience in:

- **Complex State Management:** Deepening my expertise in using React Redux to manage intricate application states effectively across various modules like scheduling and task management.

- **Building Scalable UIs:** Working with Chakra UI and React.js, I honed my skills in developing component-driven UIs that are both visually appealing and easily scalable for future features.

- **API Integration & Data Visualization:** Collaborating closely with the backend team to seamlessly integrate GoLang APIs with the React frontend, particularly for dashboard analytics and dynamic scheduling, which involved handling and presenting large datasets effectively.

- **Problem-Solving in Educational Tech:** Understanding and translating specific administrative and academic requirements into practical and intuitive frontend solutions.
