> ### **Project Background**

The **Sininis LMS** project was developed to provide a comprehensive and engaging platform for online learning. It addresses the common challenge of delivering structured educational content while offering flexibility and personalized learning paths. The primary goal was to create an intuitive system where users can easily discover, purchase, consume, and interact with various learning materials, fostering continuous skill development and knowledge acquisition through a modern, accessible interface.

---

> ### **Key Features**

As a **Frontend Developer** for **Sininis LMS**, I focused on building a highly interactive and user-centric experience for its key functionalities:

- **Dashboard & Profile:** Provides users with a personalized overview of their learning journey and allows for profile management.

- **Learning Material List:** Displays a curated list of available learning materials/courses.

- **Detailed Material Content:** Offers an in-depth view of each material, often structured with free and paid sections, encouraging exploration and purchase.

- **Material Purchase:** Seamless process for users to acquire full access to paid learning materials.

- **Review & Discussion within Material:** Integrates features for users to leave reviews and engage in discussions directly within the learning content, fostering community and feedback.

- **Personal Material Library:** A dedicated section for users to access and manage all the learning materials they own.

- **Material Storage/Favorites:** Allows users to save or favorite specific sections or entire materials for easy future reference.

---

> ### **Tech Stack**

My role as a **Frontend Developer** involved bringing the user interface of **Sininis LMS** to life using a robust and modern tech stack:

- **Frontend:**

    - **Vue.js:** Utilized for building a dynamic, reactive, and highly interactive user interface, ensuring a smooth and engaging user experience.

    - **Tailwind CSS:** Employed for rapid, utility-first styling, enabling the creation of a clean, customizable, and responsive design across all sections.

    - **Vuetify:** Integrated to provide a rich set of pre-built Material Design UI components, significantly accelerating development while maintaining a polished and professional aesthetic.

- **Backend:**

    - **Express.js:** The flexible Node.js framework served as the backbone for developing secure and scalable API endpoints, managing content, user data, and transactions.

- **Database:**

    - **PostgreSQL:** Chosen as the relational database management system for its robustness, advanced features, and strong support for complex data relationships essential for managing learning materials, user progress, and transactions.

---

> ### **Lessons Learned & Growth**

This project was an invaluable learning experience that significantly enhanced my frontend development skills, particularly in the e-learning domain. I gained deeper insights into:

- **Designing Intuitive Learning Paths:** Crafting a user experience that guides learners effectively through free and paid content sections, optimizing for content consumption and engagement.

- **Building Rich Content Interfaces:** Leveraging Vue.js and Vuetify to create complex, interactive content displays, including integrated review and discussion functionalities.

- **Integrating E-commerce within Content:** Successfully implementing the purchase flow for learning materials and managing access control based on payment status.

- **State Management for Dynamic Content:** Efficiently handling the loading, display, and interaction with various types of learning materials and user-specific data (library, favorites).
