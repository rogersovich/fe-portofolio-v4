> ### **Project Background**

The **Sahira Hotel Dashboard** project aimed to address the complexities of managing daily operations within a hotel's food and beverage department, specifically the kitchen and order fulfillment. The goal was to consolidate various tasks—from managing recipes and inventory to processing customer orders and purchase orders—into a single, intuitive platform. This system is designed to enhance efficiency, reduce manual errors, and provide clear oversight for hotel management, ultimately improving service delivery and operational control.

---

> ### **Key Features**

As a full-stack developer on this project, I implemented a range of functionalities to create a robust and integrated solution:

- **Centralized Admin Dashboard:** Provides a quick overview of daily operations, orders, and key metrics.

- **Order Management System:** Enables staff to efficiently manage incoming customer orders, track their status, and ensure timely preparation and delivery.

- **Recipe Management:** Allows for the creation, storage, and management of recipes, including ingredients and preparation steps.

- **Inventory & Store Management:** Comprehensive tools for tracking stock levels, managing product information, and overseeing store operations.

- **Purchase Order Management:** Streamlines the process of creating and managing purchase orders for ingredients and supplies.

- **User Authentication & Authorization:** Secure login (as seen in `Login.png`) and role-based access control for different user types (e.g., kitchen staff, admin).

---

> ### **Tech Stack**

As a **Fullstack Developer** for the **Sahira Hotel Dashboard**, I leveraged a modern and efficient tech stack to build both the frontend and backend:

- **Frontend:**

    - **Vue.js:** Utilized for building a dynamic, reactive, and user-friendly administrative interface.

    - **Tailwind CSS:** Employed for rapid, utility-first styling, ensuring a clean, consistent, and responsive design throughout the dashboard.

    - **Pinia:** Integrated for efficient and intuitive state management across the Vue.js application, making data flow predictable and easy to manage.

- **Backend:**

    - **Laravel:** The powerful PHP framework served as the backbone for developing robust, secure, and scalable API endpoints. It handled all business logic, data processing, authentication, and authorization.

- **Database:**

    - **MySQL:** Chosen as the relational database management system for its reliability, performance, and strong support for complex data relationships essential for managing orders, inventory, and recipes.

---

> ### **Lessons Learned & Growth**

This project provided significant growth opportunities and invaluable insights into building comprehensive enterprise solutions. I gained deeper experience in:

- **Full-Stack Integration:** Mastering the seamless communication between a Vue.js frontend (with Pinia) and a Laravel backend, optimizing API calls and data synchronization.

- **Complex Data Modeling:** Designing and implementing a robust MySQL database schema to handle interconnected data points for inventory, recipes, orders, and user roles.

- **Operational Efficiency through Software:** Understanding how well-designed software can directly impact and improve real-world business operations in a fast-paced environment like hotel F&B.

- **Building Intuitive Admin Interfaces:** Focusing on user experience from an administrative perspective to ensure the dashboard was efficient and easy for staff to use daily.
